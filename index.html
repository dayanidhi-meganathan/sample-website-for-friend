What we do

Four kinds of work that are usually stronger together. In the first conversation we'll tell you which ones you actually need, and which you don't.

PR & media relations

i.

We find the stories in your work that editors, journalists and podcast hosts will want to run, and pitch them to the right people with the right angle. When a request comes in, you're ready for it.

  • Story development
  • Press releases
  • Media outreach
  • Interview preparation

Personal branding

ii.

Your LinkedIn profile, your bio, your speaker page and the way you introduce yourself should all tell the same story. We write that story once, properly, and make every version of it match.

  • Positioning
  • LinkedIn profile
  • Short and long bios
  • Content direction

Strategic communications

iii.

Launches, announcements, new hires, difficult news. We plan what gets said, who says it and in what order, so the message lands the way you meant it.

  • Messaging framework
  • Announcement plans
  • Content calendar
  • Spokesperson notes

Reputation building

iv.

The long game. A steady record of articles, appearances, speaking slots and recognition that builds over months, so anyone who looks you up finds substance.

  • Thought-leadership articles
  • Awards and speaking submissions
  • Industry events
  • Progress reports

Who we work with

People and companies who are good at what they do, and whose public profile hasn't caught up yet.

Founders and business owners

You've built something worth talking about but have no time to talk about it. We give the business a clear public voice and make sure the founder behind it is visible too.

Professionals building an international profile

Specialists, consultants and executives who need their standing in the field to be documented and easy to find: published work, media mentions, speaking and recognition.

Businesses serving international communities

Firms and organisations in the UK whose clients come from many countries. We work in English and Romanian and understand how to speak to more than one audience at once.

03

How we work

A written plan before any publicity, and nothing goes out under your name without your sign-off.

  1. Listen

    A first conversation about your work, your goals and where you want to be seen. We ask a lot of questions.

  2. Audit

    We review everything that already exists about you online and in print: what's strong, what's missing, what's out of date.

  3. Plan

    A short written plan with priorities, channels and a realistic timeline. You approve it before we start.

  4. Build

    We do the work, report back on what happened, and adjust. Reputation compounds, so we think in months.

Why us

A good reputation is evidence that other people can find.

From law, we bring a habit of building a case: gathering proof, presenting it clearly and leaving nothing a sceptical reader could pick apart.

From client service, we bring the commercial side: knowing what clients need to hear, how they decide, and how to keep a relationship going for years.

Questions people ask first

If yours isn't here, ask us directly.

Can you guarantee press coverage?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Editors decide what they publish. What we can promise is the preparation: a story worth telling, pitched to people who cover your subject, with full visibility of who we contacted and what came back.

Do you work with individuals, or only with companies?

Both. Personal branding is half of what we do, and for many small businesses the founder's profile and the company's profile are the same project.

Which languages do you work in?

English and Romanian. That makes us a good fit for businesses and professionals who need to reach UK audiences and Romanian-speaking audiences at the same time.

Where are you based?

London. We meet clients in person in London and by video call everywhere else.
